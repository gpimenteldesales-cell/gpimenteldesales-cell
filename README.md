## About me

Hello! My name is **Guilherme Pimental de Sales**. I am 18 years old and a junior programmer, studying Digital Games Programming at SENAI in Florianopolis, Brazil.

I am the founder of **NovaVoid Studio**, where I build games and software with one goal: **innovate**. I want games that carry social critique, and functional programs that make life easier for a specific field.
